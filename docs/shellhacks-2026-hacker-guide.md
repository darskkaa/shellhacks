## Submission Process
- Teams: up to 4 hackers
- Must opt into sponsor challenge categories on Devpost submission, or they won't be considered
- Include GitHub repo link and at least one Discord tag in submission
- Provide a 3-minute video demo showing features, functionality, and impact
- One team member creates the Devpost project (use same email as ShellHacks registration) and invites teammates as collaborators
- Register with first and last name, or note names in comments (helps with prize identification)

### Judging Rounds
- **Round 1:** In-person 3-minute live demo per team, reviewed by one general judge; automatically considered for Best Overall; First-Time Hacker category evaluated if opted in and ≥50% of team qualifies
- **Round 2:** Top projects selected; 3–5 minute deep-dive demo at assigned table/room by 1:00 PM; assignments posted on Discord; late arrivals to judging areas may be disqualified

### Best Overall Prize Pool Rules
- 1st place picks first, 2nd picks second, 3rd gets remaining prize
- Team members receive the same prize — discuss choice with teammates before closing ceremony
- At least one team member must be present at Closing Ceremony to choose from the prize pool (1 minute to claim on stage) — absent teams forfeit dibs to the next-place team and receive the remaining prize
