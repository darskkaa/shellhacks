# ShellHacks 2026

Florida's Largest Hackathon — 10th Anniversary. Hosted by Florida International University (FIU), sponsored by Major League Hacking (MLH).

- **Deadline:** Sep 27, 2026 @ 11:00am EDT
- **Site:** https://shellhacks.net/
- **Format:** Public, in-person, ~1,500 attendees, 56 participants (Devpost listing)
- **Total prizes:** $41,750+ in cash + other prizes
- **Eligibility:** College students only, above legal age of majority in country of residence, all countries/territories excluding standard exceptions

## Submission Requirements
- Attach GitHub repository to submission link
- Refer to the ShellHacks 2026 Hacker Guide (linked in Devpost dashboard) for full instructions

## Judging Criteria
- Completion — how finished is the project
- Originality — is it something new and inspired
- Design — how does it look
- Technology — what technologies were used and how were they implemented
- Practicality — how easy is it for a person to use

Build a tool comparing at least two utilities' public future construction plans, flagging overlaps by physical proximity or timing. Relevant: 2024 FERC rule addressing this coordination gap.

- 2nd: Interviews + iPads
