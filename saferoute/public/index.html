The fastest route isn't always the safest. We score every alternative on crash history, flooding, construction, live crashes and hurricane surge, then pick the one you'd want a trusted driver to take.

What every route is scored on
  • 23,411 injury and fatal crashes (FDOT, 2018–2019)
  • 5,449 flooding reports (Miami-Dade 311, 2021–2023) and road elevation
  • 152 active FDOT construction projects
  • Crashes happening now, from Miami-Dade Police
  • NOAA tides, NWS alerts, NHC forecast cones, surge zones A–E, SLOSH surge depth
  • NOAA radar rainfall and 24-hour rain forecast
