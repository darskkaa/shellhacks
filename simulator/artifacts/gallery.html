Guide dog to Waymo: simulated demos

Real Edgewater, Miami streets (OpenStreetMap) and hazards (Miami-Dade 311 flooding, FDOT work zones and crashes). Only the robot hardware is simulated, in PyBullet; the car's final pull-in runs in Waymo's Waymax.

1. Obstacle course to the Waymo

The dog steers the rider around sidewalk obstacles to the door of a waiting Waymo.

2. Crossing Biscayne Blvd with traffic

The dog reads the pedestrian signal with its camera and crosses only on WALK with enough time left.

3. Flooded sidewalk, new path

At a real 311 flood-report spot the sidewalk is under water, so the dog replans and takes another way.

Built at ShellHacks 2026. Hazard data is historical and shows patterns, not predictions.
